# Maison Asrar catalog audit — 2026-11-08

## Result

ScentHunter now contains **119 Maison Asrar canonical identities** after reconciling the brand's 116-name perfume guide and the additional names present in Fragrantica.

- All 116 names in the Maison Asrar perfume guide are present.
- Three additional names — Plot Twist, Love Key and Unsolved Mystery — are also retained from the Fragrantica designer index and have retailer product pages recorded as evidence.
- The official Maison Asrar store's current collection includes Black Grapefruit as a product-card display label, while its individual product page identifies the fragrance as BLACK. The catalog now keeps one canonical identity, Black, and records Black Grapefruit as an alias rather than a duplicate.
- The store's room sprays and home fragrances are excluded from the perfume catalog.

The reseller guide explicitly states that it is not the official Maison Asrar website. Its 116-name list is used as a structured cross-check; official brand pages are preferred for product identity and details. Fragrantica is used to retain the three additional indexed names. Room sprays and reed diffusers remain outside the perfume catalog.
