# Maison Asrar catalog audit — 2026-11-08

## Result

ScentHunter already contains all **116 fragrance names** in the independent FragranceGems reference index. That index explicitly says it is not the brand's website; it states that its notes and identities were read from maisonasrar.com on 24 October 2026 and that size/type details are included only where the brand pages, product labels or retailer product data provide them.

The current ScentHunter seed has 120 Maison Asrar records. Reconciliation found one duplicate display name: the official Maison Asrar collection tile says **Black Grapefruit**, but the linked official product page is titled **Black** and describes the grapefruit/lavender/tonka fragrance. It is therefore one product, not two. The canonical identity is Black; Black Grapefruit is retained as a search alias.

Three additional Maison Asrar-labelled perfumes are listed by the Gulf Orchid retailer and were not in the 116-name guide: Love Key, Plot Twist and Unsolved Mystery. Each product page identifies Maison Asrar as the brand and explicitly states 100 ml EDP. They remain included with their retailer product URLs and 100 ml EDP variants.

## Final catalog count

- 116 names from the reference index — all present.
- 3 additional retailer-listed Maison Asrar perfumes — all present and source-linked.
- 1 duplicate display name merged into an alias (Black Grapefruit → Black).
- **119 canonical Maison Asrar identities** after reconciliation.
- The eight room sprays and eight reed diffusers shown separately by the brand are home-fragrance products, not perfume identities, and remain outside this catalog.
