# Velixir catalogue reconciliation — 2026-11-08

## Sources and scope
- Official brand storefront: https://velixirparfums.com/collections/all
- Official Adonis product page: https://velixirparfums.com/products/adonis
- Official Comus product page: https://velixirparfums.com/products/velixir-parfums-comus-extrait-de-parfum-for-unisex
- Consolidated range and size cross-check (independent reseller guide, not the official site): https://velixirperfumes.eu/perfumes/
- Fragrantica designer index: https://www.fragrantica.com/designers/Velixir.html

The official storefront currently lists 29 products across two pages: 28 perfumes plus a Discovery Set. The Discovery Set is a sample set, not a perfume identity, and is intentionally excluded from the perfume catalog. Fragrantica lists 24 perfumes; it does not yet include the four newer Extrait line identities shown by the brand.

## Identity reconciliation
All 28 perfume names from the consolidated range are already represented in ScentHunter:
Adonis, Aphrodite, Apollo, Ares, Artemis, Asteria, Athena, Comus, Concordia, Demeter, Ether, Galatea, Harmonia, Helios, Hera, Himeros, Icarus, Morpheus, Narcisus, Nyx, Orion, Orpheus, Paladin, Panacea, Persephone, Poseidon, Selene, Uranus.

No additional perfume identity was required. The four Extrait line products — Comus, Ether, Orpheus and Panacea — are retained even though the current Fragrantica index lists only 24.

## Variant reconciliation
The brand's EDP pages show 35 ml and 100 ml options; the four Extrait products are listed as 100 ml. The consolidated guide cross-checks the full range and states 24 EDPs in 35/100 ml and four Extraits in 100 ml.

- 24 Eau de Parfum identities × 2 sizes = 48 variants.
- 4 Extrait de Parfum identities × 1 size = 4 variants.
- Total: 52 variants.

ScentHunter previously had zero Velixir variants. This update adds all 52 and stores the size list on each product and family registry entry.

## Alias handling
- Narcissus is retained as a spelling alias for the brand's official spelling Narcisus.
- Harmony is retained as a spelling alias for Harmonia.

## Validation
- 28 canonical perfume identities.
- 52 size/concentration variants.

- Availability is not inferred from the catalog; the official store currently shows items as sold out/unavailable.
