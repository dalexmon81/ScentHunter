# Paris Corner catalogue audit — batch 1 — 2026-11-08

## Scope and sources

- Official Paris Corner store, all-products collection: https://www.pariscornerperfumes.com/collections/all (currently 255 products across Paris Corner and its associated labels).
- Official Paris Corner core-brand collection: https://www.pariscornerperfumes.com/collections/paris-corner (currently 104 products).
- Official Ministry of Oud collection and product pages.
- Official Ministry of Gourmand collection and product pages.
- Fragrantica's Paris Corner designer page as a secondary cross-check.

The ScentHunter seed begins with 246 Paris Corner identities. This count includes the wider Paris Corner product family and associated lines, so it is not directly comparable to the 104-product core Paris Corner collection or the 255-product all-products collection.

The Paris Corner family registry has 274 entries. Fourteen names in that registry were not represented as exact product identities in the seed after normalizing accents and punctuation. Twelve are distinct product names with official product pages; two are spelling variants of existing identities.
