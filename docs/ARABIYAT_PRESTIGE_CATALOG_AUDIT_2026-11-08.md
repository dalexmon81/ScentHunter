# Arabiyat Prestige catalog audit — 2026-11-08

## Verified additions

The seed contained 118 Arabiyat Prestige identities. The family registry had five additional distinct names not yet promoted to the catalog, plus three spelling/display-name differences. Three of the five additional names have official My Perfumes product pages with explicit EDP 100 ml details:
- Attar Al Youm Black
- Musk Al Youm
- Oud Al Youm

These three identities and their 100 ml EDP variants are added. The following spelling differences are aliases rather than separate products:
- Nyla Vani-elle → Nyla Vani-Elle
- Asha'a Mauve → Ash'aa Mauve
- Noire → Noirè

Two remaining family-registry names — Red Oud and Al Shujae — still need an individual official product page or reliable primary evidence. They are not promoted in this change.
