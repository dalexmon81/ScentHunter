# Khadlaj reconciliation — 2026-11-08

## Scope

This branch rebases the Khadlaj work onto `catalog/mykonos-rebased-99-102-2026-11-08`, preserving the Mykonos work and the other catalog data already on that base. It does not merge or alter `main`.

## Reconciled catalog

- 210 Khadlaj canonical identities.
- 212 explicit variants.
- 96 visible official perfume-spray cards reconciled from the official collection.
- 36 visible official concentrated-oil products represented.
- 42 official La Fede EDP collection products represented.
- 147 Fragrantica name records retained as identity leads; Fragrantica is not used as evidence for sizes or concentrations.
- 8 Shiyaaka identities remain grouped as a separate line.

## Variant policy

Every variant row has a `source_evidence_url`. Product-level URLs are not treated as variant evidence unless the exact product page or collection card supports the size and concentration. Concentration is left blank where official sources conflict, and perfume oils remain distinct from alcohol-based sprays.

## Important identity and source decisions

- Private Blend Tobac Extra is distinct from Private Blend.
- Saqr Al Badiya has a confirmed 100 ml size but no inferred concentration because the official page is inconsistent.
- Hareem Al Sultan Gold is a 35 ml Perfume Oil.
- Musk Pure Musk Blend Creation of Iqbal is an alias of Musk Pure Musk Blend.
- Code Viola Nectar belongs to the La Fede family and is not duplicated.
- Rose & Romance and Opulence Bleu have direct variant evidence sources rather than Fragrantica links.
