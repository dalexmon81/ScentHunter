# Maison Asrar catalogue reconciliation — 8 November 2026

## Sources and scope

- Official Maison Asrar store: https://maisonasrar.com/collections/all
- Official Black product page: https://maisonasrar.com/products/black-eau-de-perfum-100ml
- Fragrantica designer listing: https://www.fragrantica.com/designers/MAISON-ASRAR.html
- Independent EU range guide (not official): https://maisonasrarperfumes.eu/perfumes/
- Gulf Orchid product pages for Lock & Key and recent retailer-supported launches.
- The Alternative Beauty and Youshah product pages for Amal and Asala.

The official store's all-products page mixes fragrances with room sprays and home fragrances. Its product count is not a perfume count. The independent EU guide explicitly says it is not the official Maison Asrar website and is run by an independent reseller. It lists 116 perfume identities and reports size/type only where supported by brand pages, label photos or shop product data.

## Identity reconciliation

- The 116 names in the independent EU guide are represented in the catalog.
- Love Key, Plot Twist and Unsolved Mystery remain distinct identities with Gulf Orchid product-page evidence.
- Six additional names are retained from Fragrantica: Amal, Asala, Red Carpet, Runway Girl, Shaheen Black and Smokey Eyes.
- The false “Black Grapefruit” split is removed. The official collection entry labelled “BLACK GRAPEFRUIT · LAVENDER · TONKA” links to the official product detail page titled “BLACK”; grapefruit, lavender and tonka are notes, not a second perfume.
- “Silver Elixer” remains the catalog name with “Silver Elixir” as a spelling alias. “Rose” receives “Rosé” as an alias.

The resulting catalog contains **125 Maison Asrar identities**. This is a cross-source identity union, not a claim that all 125 are currently in stock.

## Variant reconciliation

- **105 variants** are supported by the independent EU guide. It identifies 70 as Eau de Parfum; for the other 35 the concentration remains unspecified.
- **3 existing 100 ml EDP variants** remain for Love Key, Plot Twist and Unsolved Mystery, each supported by its Gulf Orchid product page.
- **5 additional 100 ml EDP variants** are supported by individual retailer pages: Amal, Asala, Red Carpet, Runway Girl and Smokey Eyes.
- **Shaheen Black remains name-only**. Fragrantica lists it under Maison Asrar, but the Gulf Orchid product page labels Shaheen Black as Gulf Orchid. No size or concentration is assigned until attribution is resolved.

The final Maison Asrar catalog contains **113 variants**. Every variant carries its own `source_evidence_url`; the independent guide is clearly identified as non-official evidence.

## Product-type exclusions

Room sprays, reed diffusers, home fragrances and bundles are not imported as perfume identities or bottle-size variants. They require separate product-type handling.

## Validation

- 125 Maison Asrar canonical identities.
- 113 Maison Asrar variants.
- No separate Black Grapefruit identity.
- No duplicate product IDs, duplicate variant IDs or orphan variants.
- Every Maison Asrar variant has variant-level source evidence.
- Concentration remains unspecified where the independent guide does not state it.
- Shaheen Black remains name-only pending attribution confirmation.
