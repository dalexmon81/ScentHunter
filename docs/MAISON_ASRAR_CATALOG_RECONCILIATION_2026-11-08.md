# Maison Asrar catalogue reconciliation — 2026-11-08

## Sources and scope
- Official Maison Asrar store: https://maisonasrar.com/collections/all
- Official Black product page: https://maisonasrar.com/products/black-eau-de-perfum-100ml
- Fragrantica designer listing: https://www.fragrantica.com/designers/MAISON-ASRAR.html
- Independent EU range guide (not official): https://maisonasrarperfumes.eu/perfumes/

The official store's all-products page currently reports 130 mixed products; its navigation separates fragrances, room sprays and home fragrances. Do not treat the 130 total as a count of perfumes. The independent range guide lists 116 perfume identities and says its notes come from the brand pages; type and size are included where supported by the brand page, label photo or shop product data. Fragrantica reports 119 perfume records and includes several additional legacy names.

## Identity reconciliation
- All 116 names in the EU guide are already present in the original ScentHunter Maison Asrar catalog.
- Three additional names already in ScentHunter are also listed by Fragrantica: Love Key, Plot Twist and Unsolved Mystery.
- Five Fragrantica names absent from the seed are added as name-only, source-listed records: Asala, Red Carpet, Runway Girl, Shaheen Black and Smokey Eyes. No size or concentration is inferred for them.
- The seed's “Black Grapefruit” entry was a false product split. The official brand product page title is “Black”; grapefruit, lavender and tonka are its notes. The incorrect Black Grapefruit identity is removed. It is not retained as an alias.
- “Silver Elixer” is retained as the catalog name, with “Silver Elixir” as a spelling alias. “Rose” receives “Rosé” as an alias for the Fragrantica spelling.

The resulting 124 identities are a cross-source union, not a claim that all 124 are currently in stock or simultaneously marketed.

## Variant reconciliation
The independent guide provides explicit sizes for 105 of the 116 perfume names. Those sizes are recorded as variants. For 70 names the guide explicitly identifies Eau de Parfum; those variants are marked EDP. For the other 35 size-known names, size is recorded but concentration remains unspecified. Eleven names in the guide have no explicit size in the guide and therefore receive no variant from this update.

- 105 variants added for the 116 guide-listed perfumes where size is explicitly stated.
- 3 existing 100 ml EDP variants retained for Love Key, Plot Twist and Unsolved Mystery.
- 108 Maison Asrar variants total.
- Five Fragrantica-only legacy names remain without variants.

## Product-type exclusions
Room sprays and reed diffusers are not imported as perfume identities or bottle-size variants. The Discovery/collection pages are treated as product scope only where they identify a fragrance, not a home-fragrance product.

## Validation
- 124 Maison Asrar canonical identities.
- 108 Maison Asrar variants.
- The false “Black Grapefruit” split is removed; the official “Black” perfume is retained.
- No duplicate product IDs, duplicate variant IDs, or orphan variants.
- Unknown sizes and concentrations remain unknown; no variants are invented for the five Fragrantica-only names.
