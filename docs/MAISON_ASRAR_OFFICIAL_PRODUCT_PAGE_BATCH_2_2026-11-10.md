# Maison Asrar official product-page verification — batch 2

## Scope

Seven existing Maison Asrar catalog identities were checked against individual pages linked from the brand's official product collection.

## Product-page provenance

- Dubai Milano — https://maisonasrar.com/products/dubai-milano
- DXB — https://maisonasrar.com/products/dxb
- Emessa — https://maisonasrar.com/products/maison-asrar-emessa-eau-de-parfum-for-women
- Fairy Tale — https://maisonasrar.com/products/fairy-tale-eau-de-perfum-100ml
- Fakhama — https://maisonasrar.com/products/maison-asrar-fakhama-eau-de-parfum-100-ml-for-women
- Faris Al Arab — https://maisonasrar.com/products/faris-al-arab
- Fresh Iris — https://maisonasrar.com/products/fresh-iris-maison-asrar-eau-de-perfum-100-ml

All seven existing canonical IDs were retained and upgraded to individual product-page provenance. The official collection labels the seven as Eau de Parfum.

## Variant decisions

The official product URLs or collection product labels explicitly identify 100 ml for Emessa, Fairy Tale, Fakhama and Fresh Iris. Those four products received one 100 ml EDP variant each and matching family-registry formats.

The official pages for Dubai Milano, DXB and Faris Al Arab do not establish a bottle size in the accessible product URL or collection label. Their EDP concentration was recorded, but no size or variant was inferred.

## Brand-attribution exceptions

The collection page labels Emerald as “Gulf Orchid Emerald”, and the Flair product URL contains “gulf-orchid-flaire”. These two records were deliberately not upgraded in this Maison Asrar batch. Their brand ownership needs a separate source-level reconciliation to avoid assigning a Gulf Orchid product to Maison Asrar.

## Exclusions

No prices, stock, availability or retailer offers were added. No GTIN was added without barcode evidence.
