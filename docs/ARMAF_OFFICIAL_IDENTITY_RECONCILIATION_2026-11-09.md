## Existing products upgraded

The three existing Club de Nuit identities (Intense Man, Man and Oud) retain their product IDs and receive the official EU concentration and 105 ml size evidence. Their matching 105 ml variants are attached to the existing product IDs.

## Reconciliation safeguards

- No prices, stock, availability or retailer offers were introduced.
- No unrelated brand rows were imported from source branches.
- Conflicting Check Mate and French Kiss product IDs were not both retained.
- Official source URLs and alternate spellings are preserved as provenance/aliases.
- A regression test checks canonical uniqueness, family-registry coverage, source URLs, variant sizes, and ID integrity.
