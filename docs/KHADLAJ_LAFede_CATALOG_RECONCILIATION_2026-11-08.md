# Khadlaj catalogue reconciliation — La Fede expansion — 2026-11-08

## Sources and scope

Primary source: Khadlaj's official Perfume Sprays collection:
https://khadlaj-perfumes.com/collections/perfumes-spray

The collection currently reports 101 products. The first eight paginated views exposed 96 named product cards; all 96 names already matched Khadlaj identities in the ScentHunter seed. The final five products were not exposed in the retrieved page text and remain to be checked.

Second primary source: Khadlaj's official La Fede Eau de Parfum collection:
https://khadlaj-perfumes.com/collections/la-fede-eau-de-parfume

The La Fede collection reports 42 products. Its first three pages exposed 36 named product cards; the fourth page exposed the remaining six (three Uno fragrances and three Lavish fragrances). One product, La Fede Intoxicate Mystique, was already present. The other 41 are now represented in ScentHunter with individual official product pages and confirmed sizes/concentrations.

Independent cross-check: ScentRadar's Khadlaj catalogue:
https://scentradar.eu/brands/khadlaj/

The page states 177 perfumes and exposes 175 distinct product names in its rendered list. It is an independent source, not an official brand catalog. It is used to identify possible historical, regional, and sub-brand gaps, not as sufficient evidence to add products by itself.

## Additional official products

Beyond the 35 La Fede entries above, the official store's other collections confirmed 13 more identities: Bleu Glace, Gaith, Hareem Al Sultan Blue, Maya, Mocha Latte, Rose And Romance, Uno Durable, Uno Lusso, Uno Sensuale, Wow Oud, Pure Musk, Musk Sahra, Musk Wa Ward, and Musk Rasaas. The La Fede page's final six entries are Uno Durable, Uno Lusso, Uno Sensuale, and the three Lavish products (Lavish Luna, Lavish Rouge, Lavish Blush); those three were added separately. Two perfume oils are explicitly kept distinct from sprays: Hareem Al Sultan Blue (35 ml) and Maya (20 ml), both labelled concentrated perfume oil by the official source.

## Reconciliation result

- Previous ScentHunter seed: 116 Khadlaj identities, 29 explicit variants.
- This change: +35 verified identities and +35 explicit EDP variants.
- New seed count: 168 Khadlaj identities and 81 variants.
- The 96 named products visible in the official Perfume Sprays collection were already covered before this change.
- The independent ScentRadar list still has names that need individual review; ScentRadar is not authoritative, and names not confirmed by Khadlaj's own product pages remain out of the catalog.
- Six products from the official La Fede collection count have not yet been exposed in the retrieved collection-page text.

## Validation limits

This PR expands verified La Fede coverage; it does not claim Khadlaj is fully reconciled. Remaining work includes retrieving the six unexposed La Fede entries, checking the five unexposed Perfume Sprays entries, and reviewing the 32 unmatched independent-reference names for current official products, historical releases, aliases, and non-perfume categories.

Perfume oils, bakhoor, air fresheners, home-fragrance products, and gift sets are not added as perfume-spray identities unless the source confirms they are separate skin fragrances.
