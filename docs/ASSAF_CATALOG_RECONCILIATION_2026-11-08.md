# Assaf catalogue reconciliation — 2026-11-08

## Sources

- Official Assaf store: https://assaf.ae/products and its Men, Women, Lady, Frankel, Arrogate and Enable perfume collections.
- Independent EU Assaf guide: https://assafperfumes.eu/de/perfumes/
- Fragrantica Assaf designer page as a secondary reference.

The independent guide explicitly states it is not the official Assaf website. It lists 140 perfume identities and says product details were checked against assaf.ae (and for some items 3saf.com) on 24 October 2026. It is a useful cross-check, not an official master catalog.

## Reconciliation result

- ScentHunter seed: 140 Assaf canonical identities.
- Independent reference: 140 names.
- All 140 reference names are present in the seed, matching canonical names or aliases.
- The family registry also contains 140 Assaf entries, with full name coverage.
- No new product identities were required by this audit.

## Data-quality finding: variants

The catalog has eight Assaf variant rows across seven products. The other 133 identities do not yet have a structured variant row, even though the independent reference provides size/concentration information for many of them.

This is a variant-data completeness issue, not a missing-name issue. The next Assaf task is a separate variant harvest using individual official product pages; do not copy the third-party size/concentration data into production variants without checking the corresponding brand product page.

## Scope limits

Fragrantica currently lists 78 Assaf perfumes, while the independent EU guide lists 140 identities. The larger count includes additional brand collections and releases not necessarily represented in Fragrantica. The 140-name reconciliation is therefore based on the broader independent list, with the official Assaf site as the preferred source for subsequent product-detail verification.
