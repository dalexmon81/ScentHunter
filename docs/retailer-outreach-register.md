## Rules for status changes

- Received means only that an email or ticket was acknowledged.
- Awaiting response means no substantive reply was found in the mailbox at the last check.
- Approved may be used only after explicit written permission or confirmed official affiliate/feed access, with its scope and conditions recorded.
- Record separately whether permission covers product facts, prices, availability, periodic refresh, feed/API access, images, descriptions, logos, territories, attribution, and commercial use.
- Never treat a retailer's public product page, an auto-reply, an affiliate-network account activation, or a connector being present in code as permission to collect or publish retailer offers.
