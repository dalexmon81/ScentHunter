## Data decisions

- All seven products keep their existing canonical product IDs.
- One explicit size/concentration variant is recorded per product.
- Product formats_ml and matching family-registry rows are aligned.
- Existing canonical source_url values are preserved for compatibility; official European e-shop product URLs are stored as source_evidence_url and on the corresponding variant.
- No GTINs were shown on these official pages; GTIN fields are omitted.
- Price, stock, availability and retail offers are excluded from canonical data.
