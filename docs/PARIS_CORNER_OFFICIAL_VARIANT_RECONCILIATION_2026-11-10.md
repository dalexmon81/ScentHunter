## Data decisions

- Reused the existing canonical product IDs. No duplicate products were created.
- Added one size/concentration variant to each of the four existing products.
- Updated the product formats_ml and corresponding family-registry rows.
- Added official e-shop URLs as product-level evidence.
- No GTINs were present on these official pages; GTIN fields are deliberately omitted.
- Retail pricing, sale status, stock and availability are deliberately excluded from the canonical catalog.

## Follow-up items

The pages for Arwa, Atomise Intensive Men, Charuto Regal Reserve and other audited Paris Corner identities do not yet have a verified official volume/concentration pairing in this pass. They remain without variants until product-level evidence is found. This audit does not claim the Paris Corner range is complete.
