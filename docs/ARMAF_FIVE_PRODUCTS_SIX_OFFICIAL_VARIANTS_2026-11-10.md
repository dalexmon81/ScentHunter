## Source discrepancy retained transparently

The Armaf EU Pure Parfum page is titled “Pure Parfum” and lists 150 ml and 200 ml, but its descriptive body also contains an “Eau de Parfum” label. The existing Armaf.com product page explicitly identifies this canonical product as Parfum. The variants therefore retain the existing Parfum classification and the EU page is used only for size evidence. This discrepancy is recorded rather than silently changing concentration.
